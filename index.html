Card staleness

A card flags when its newest Current Action entry is more than two business days old. Marginal and drift are shown apart on purpose — collapsing them into one percentage can invert which rosters look healthy.

Clean — logged within 2 business days Marginal — exactly 3 days, one over the line Drift — 4 days or more
